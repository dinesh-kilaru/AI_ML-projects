Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) Summary


Dataset Overview
The dataset contains 545 entries with 13 columns, including numerical and object (categorical) types.
No missing values were identified, ensuring data quality for analysis.
Numerical Feature Analysis
Price: Ranges from 1.75 million to 13.3 million, with a mean of 4.77 million and a median of 4.34 million, indicating a slight positive skew. High variance (3.5e+12) and standard deviation (1.87 million) suggest a wide distribution of prices.
Area: Ranges from 1650 to 16200 sq.ft., with a mean of 5150 sq.ft. and median of 4600 sq.ft., also showing a positive skew. Variance (4.7e+06) and standard deviation (2170 sq.ft.) indicate significant spread.
Other numerical features (bedrooms, bathrooms, stories, parking) show typical ranges for housing data, with bathrooms having a higher skewness.
Outlier Detection
Box plots revealed significant outliers, particularly in price and area, suggesting potential high-value properties or data entry anomalies. These outliers were not explicitly handled in the modeling phase but were noted.
Correlation Analysis
A heatmap and correlation values with 'price' were generated:
price shows the strongest positive correlation with area (0.54), bathrooms (0.52), stories (0.42), parking (0.38), and bedrooms (0.37).
These correlations indicate that larger areas, more bathrooms, more stories, and parking facilities generally lead to higher prices.
